A journey into the inner self

An indie game inspired by Carl Jung’s psychology, specifically the ‘individuation process’
A hero who faces their own shadow in the dark, confronts long-hidden feelings, and begins a deep journey inward

A place where you meet
the self you want to hide.

You try to move forward,
but a nameless shadow
keeps getting in your way.
The hero stands up to it,
determined to learn
what it really is.

A conversation between
the rational and emotional self

Suddenly,
the hero becomes two.
Which one
is real?

Reaching a whole self

Grown through
the journey so far,
the hero finally
comes face to face
with their true self.

The Guide

Everyone has a small presence
that lights the way through the dark.
Sometimes it leads the way;
sometimes it simply stays by your side.

Persona “Me”

Gender, age and appearance unknown
A whole person, shown without visual cues

True to the theme of “dreams”: a dream anyone can have,
a story where anyone can be the hero
